Under Section 52 of the CGST Act, e-commerce operators must collect Tax Collected at Source (TCS) at 1% (comprising 0.5% CGST + 0.5% SGST for intrastate transactions, or 1% IGST for interstate transactions) on the net value of taxable supplies facilitated through their platform. The 'net value' is calculated as the aggregate value of taxable supplies of goods or services minus the aggregate value of returned taxable supplies in a given month. This GST TCS operates entirely independently of Income Tax TDS and is deposited against the vendor's unique GSTIN. Compliance and payout discrepancies frequently arise if TCS is applied inconsistently, if the vendor's GST registration status changes mid-settlement cycle, or if the vendor is an exempted, unregistered person making intra-state supplies (not subject to TCS), leading to mismatches between the marketplace ledger and the actual cash settlement.
