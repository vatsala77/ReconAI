# Context: CGST Section 52 - E-commerce TCS & GSTR-8 Compliance

## Legal Framework & Core Mandate
- **Statutory Provision:** Section 52 of the CGST Act.
- **Applicability:** Electronic Commerce Operators (ECOs) / E-commerce platforms.
- **TCS Rate:** 1% of the net value of taxable supplies.
- **Filing Frequency:** Monthly.
- **Reporting Return:** GSTR-8 filing.
- **Mandatory Action:** Collect TCS, report via GSTR-8, and deposit funds with the government.

## Financial Formula
- **Base Metric:** Net Value of Taxable Supplies.
- **Formula Components:** Gross Transaction Value MINUS Sales Returns MINUS Discounts.
- **Expected TCS Calculation:** 1% × Net Value of Taxable Supplies.

## Root Causes of TCS Reconciliation Mismatches
A mismatch between expected TCS (1% of gross transaction value) and actually reported TCS occurs due to:
1. **Taxability Classification Errors:** Incorrectly classifying a taxable supply as exempt or non-taxable.
2. **Net Value Calculation Errors:** Failing to accurately deduct sales returns or applicable discounts from the gross value.
3. **Filing Timeline Gaps:** Delayed or incomplete transactional data entry for the given monthly tax period.

## Legal and Financial Risks
- **Under-Reporting Impact:** Creates an immediate compliance and financial liability for the e-commerce platform.
- **Statutory Penalties:** Attracts interest charges and financial penalties under GST law if discrepancies are not corrected before the official filing deadline.
