# Context: Razorpay Route Transfer & UTR Bank Settlement

## Summary
Route transfers show as "settled" or "processed" once Razorpay initiates them. However, actual vendor bank credit requires 1-2 business days via IMPS, NEFT, or RTGS. Tracking and confirmation rely entirely on the Unique Transaction Reference (UTR) number issued by the receiving bank.

## Key Metadata
- **System Owner:** Razorpay (Route feature)
- **Primary Tracking Identifier:** UTR (Unique Transaction Reference) number
- **Issuing Entity for UTR:** The receiving bank
- **Standard Settlement Timeframe:** 1-2 business days
- **Supported Payment Networks:** IMPS, NEFT, RTGS

## System Status Meanings
- **"Settled" / "Processed" Status:** Confirms Razorpay initiated the transfer. It does NOT guarantee the funds have hit the vendor's account yet.
- **UTR-Confirmed Status:** Confirms actual bank settlement and credit.

## Troubleshooting: Missing Credit After 1-2 Business Days
If dashboard status is "settled" but no bank credit/UTR exists after 2 business days, the cause is one of the following:
1. Delayed inter-bank settlement cycles.
2. Bank-side processing failure.
3. Reconciliation gap between Route's internal ledger and actual bank confirmation.

## Troubleshooting: Amount Discrepancies
If the Route transfer amount does not match the actual UTR-credited amount, look for:
1. Bank-side deduction of intermediary charges.
2. Incorrect bank account number or IFSC mapping (requires manual investigation).
