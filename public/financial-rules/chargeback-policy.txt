When a customer initiates a transaction dispute or chargeback, Razorpay or a connected platform flags the specific transfer or settlement on hold to mitigate risk. The dispute resolution timeline does not follow the RBI's 5-to-7 day failed transaction rule; instead, standard card network (Visa, Mastercard, RuPay) and banking investigations take anywhere between 30 to 120 days for final closure. If the tracking window passes and a transfer remains locked under a hold status, it typically flags an edge case requiring manual operations review. This scenario signifies either that the financial institution has extended the fraud/retrieval audit timeline, or the platform’s webhook failed to auto-trigger the release sequence after the merchant won the dispute representment. Platforms should proactively surface active dispute identifiers, remaining response windows, and documentation requirements directly to impacted vendors to preserve payout transparency.
