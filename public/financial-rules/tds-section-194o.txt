Under Section 194-O of the Income Tax Act, e-commerce operators must deduct Tax Deducted at Source (TDS) on the gross amount of sales or services facilitated through their digital platform. The standard TDS rate is 0.1%, effective from October 1, 2024. If the e-commerce participant (seller) fails to provide a PAN or Aadhaar, the TDS rate increases to a punitive rate of 5%. A threshold exemption of ₹5 lakh per financial year applies exclusively to sellers who are resident individuals or Hindu Undivided Families (HUFs). For all other entity types (such as Companies, Partnership Firms, or LLPs), TDS applies from the first transaction without any threshold limit. The platform operator must deduct this tax at the time of crediting the seller's account or making the payment, whichever is earlier, and deposit it with the government. Any failure, omission, or incorrect calculation of this deduction results in an accounting settlement discrepancy between the platform's expected marketplace ledger and the actual cash payout received by the vendor.
